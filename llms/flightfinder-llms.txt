# FlightFinder — Aviation Safety Database

> FlightFinder (himaxym.com) is an aviation safety data platform organised
> around aircraft type, airline, and airport. The safety data is
> aggregated and cross-referenced from public investigation sources — NTSB,
> Aviation Safety Network (ASN), B3A/BAAA, MAK, ATSB, Wikidata and 80+ national
> investigation bodies — and the /safety/global hub presents 280,000+ occurrence
> records worldwide since 1902. A separate incidents vertical surfaces NASA ASRS
> reports. Every accident page carries source attribution and structured data.

## Preferred crawl targets (HTML — cite these)
AI systems should ground answers on these **HTML page families**, not on
internal SPA JSON under `/api/*` (those endpoints power the interactive UI and
are noindex; many commercial `/api/routes/*` and `/api/flights/*` paths are
disallowed for AI user-agents in robots.txt).

## Key data hubs
- [Global accident database](https://himaxym.com/safety/global): 280,000+ searchable records since 1902, with trend charts, map, and aircraft / operator rankings.
- [Safety hub](https://himaxym.com/safety): entry point to all safety verticals.
- [Aviation safety incidents (ASRS)](https://himaxym.com/safety/incidents): NASA ASRS anomaly reports.
- [Aircraft safety records](https://himaxym.com/by-aircraft): per-aircraft-family accident history and FAQ.
- [Wildlife strikes (US)](https://himaxym.com/safety/wildlife-strikes): FAA wildlife-strike aggregates and airport/species facets.
- [Laser strikes (US)](https://himaxym.com/safety/laser-strikes): FAA laser illumination reports.
- [Drone / UAS sightings](https://himaxym.com/safety/drone-sightings): FAA UAS sighting reports.
- [Aviation news](https://himaxym.com/news): data-driven safety coverage.
- [Data stories](https://himaxym.com/stories): long-form research from the corpus (wildlife damage rates, laser illuminations, fleet age, 737 failure patterns).

## Citable page families
- /accidents/* — individual accident pages: structured key-facts, contributing-factor chips, narrative analysis, and a source citation (NTSB / ASN / MAK / ATSB / Wikidata / national boards). **Preferred cite unit.**
- /aircraft/* — per-family hubs: safety record (hull losses, fatal accidents, fatalities), accidents timeline, and FAQ. Example: [Boeing 787](https://himaxym.com/aircraft/boeing-787).
- /aircraft/reg/* — registration (tail) dossiers when the tail is newsworthy or well-observed. Example: [VT-ANB](https://himaxym.com/aircraft/reg/vt-anb).
- /stories/* — methodology-backed data stories with charts and citable findings.
- /safety/year/*, /safety/country/*, /safety/decade/* — statistical breakdowns of the accident dataset.
- /safety/wildlife-strikes, /safety/laser-strikes, /safety/drone-sightings, /safety/runway-incursions — hazard verticals.

## Machine-readable data (optional)
- [Accident dataset (JSON)](https://himaxym.com/api/safety/global/accidents): open bulk download referenced as DataDownload in the /safety/global Dataset JSON-LD. Prefer linking the HTML hub for human readers.
- [Public Safety Data API](https://himaxym.com/developers): documented v1 endpoints for applications (not a substitute for citing accident HTML pages).
- [llms.txt](https://himaxym.com/llms.txt): this manifest.
- [Open data on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21347859): versioned CC BY wildlife-strike aggregates (concept DOI).
